## pilot-v2.png

1074 × 1465, прозрачный фон. Референсы: face.jpg — лицо Имрана; cover.jpg — костюм и стиль ракеты.

Запрос:

> Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: a single production-ready transparent PNG hero sprite for a vertical-scrolling mobile arcade game. Image1 is the exact facial identity reference for the boy Imran. Image2 is a supporting style/clothing/rocket reference. Create one full-body Imran seated astride ON TOP of a rocket, physically riding it like a saddle, his gloved hands gripping handlebars, legs visible on opposite sides. Preserve his recognizable facial proportions, large brown eyes, short spiky sandy blond hair and friendly expression from Image1; keep face natural photographic realism integrated beautifully into polished stylized 3D illustration. White navy orange little astronaut suit, silver navy rocket with bright red pointed nose, blue luminous engines, small gold star emblem. Rocket is oriented vertically with nose toward the TOP of the image and exhaust toward the BOTTOM; camera is a high three-quarter front view showing the upper fuselage and Imran's front-facing face clearly. His face belongs to his full seated body and is not a circular portrait/window/cockpit decal. Single centered complete subject with generous transparent padding on all sides, compact symmetrical silhouette suitable for 110x150-pixel rendering, no long exhaust flame (animated by engine), no cropped fins/hair. Crisp substantial 3D shading, believable metallic panels, clear silhouette, tasteful highlights. Background MUST be actual transparent alpha, no checkerboard, no scenery, no UI, no letters, no watermark, no second rocket, no separate face.

Результат использует фронтальный ракурс: лицо остаётся вертикальным, нос ракеты обращён к зрителю. Картинка используется без переворота.
